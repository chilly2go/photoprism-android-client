- **Review:** the new "Review" in the menu lists the photos PhotoPrism is unsure about, mostly of low quality, with their number next to it. Approve them to keep them in the library as they are, or move them to the archive, one by one in the viewer or selected together.
- **Archive:** shows how many photos it holds. Restoring and deleting forever ask first, and deleting says it can't be undone.
- Every archive and review action tells how it went, also when it failed. Moving to the archive and restoring can be undone right away; approving can't, PhotoPrism has no way back.
- Review and archive only show up for accounts allowed to use them.
