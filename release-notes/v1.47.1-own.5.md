- **People:** the new "People" in the menu shows everyone the library knows, favorites first, with the number of photos. A tap opens their photos, a long press offers renaming, favorite and hiding. Renaming to a name somebody else already has asks first, because the two are then merged into one.
- **Who is this?** The "New" tab lists faces nobody is named for yet, the biggest groups first. A tap asks who this is, with the known names suggested, and shows the photos of the face if needed. A face can also be hidden.
- **Names are shared:** people and their names belong to the whole library, so a name given on one phone shows up for every account. Naming a face can't be undone in the app; a wrong name is fixed by renaming or on the web.
- **Hidden people and faces:** hiding can be undone right away, and the eye at the top lists hidden ones to show them again.
- Accounts which may only view people see the lists without these actions.
