- **People in the photo:** the new face button in the viewer frames the faces of the photo, labelled with their names, and lists the people at the bottom. The frames follow zooming and panning, and the setting stays on while swiping through photos.
- Tap a face to see the photos of the person, say it is someone else, remove the name, confirm a suggested name or mark it as not a face (with undo). An unnamed face says "Who is this?" and can be named right there.
- **Mark a missed face:** place a frame on the face and give it a name.
- Names are shared by everyone using the library: naming a face can name other photos of the same unnamed face too, and a marked face can't be deleted, only marked as not a face.
- Without permission to change people in the library, the faces are only shown.
