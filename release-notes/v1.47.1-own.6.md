- **No more doubled photos:** when an app uploads a photo without access to its location, Android blanks the location in the file. The upstream app uploaded such copies, and this app, which uploads the photo with its location, didn't recognize them, so the same photo ended up in the library twice. The picker and the upload queue now recognize such copies, so the photo counts as already uploaded and is not uploaded again.
- Photos already uploaded twice stay doubled; they need to be cleaned up on the server.
